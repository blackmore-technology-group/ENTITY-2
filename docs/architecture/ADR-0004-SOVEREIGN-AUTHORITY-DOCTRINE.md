# ADR-0004 — Sovereign Authority Doctrine and Provider Non-Authority
Status: Accepted
Baseline: SERS-ENTITY-003 v2.2, Section 172

## Problem
Infrastructure providers may possess, store, host, process, index, back up or technically access Entity-controlled information. Those technical relationships must not silently become sovereign authority, ownership, consent authority, licensing authority, governance authority or economic participation.

## Decision
ENTITY SHALL model infrastructure relationships and authority relationships separately. Evidence for possession, custody, storage control or processing authority SHALL NOT establish a higher authority relationship unless an explicit governed record establishes that authority.

Provider substitution SHALL preserve the sovereign Entity root and authority/economic semantics unless an explicit governed authority transition is separately recorded.
